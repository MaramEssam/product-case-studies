🚀 **Product Management Case Study #21: Solving the Mystery of Declining Customer Retention**

What happens when a food delivery company acquires more customers but struggles to grow its revenue? 🤔

In this case study, I explored a business challenge that highlights an important Product Management principle: **growth in acquisition doesn't always translate into sustainable growth.**

📊 **The Business Challenge**

The company was facing several concerning signals:

📉 30-day customer retention dropped from **40% to 28%**.

📈 New customer acquisition increased by **25%**.

💰 Marketing spending increased by **30%**, while revenue remained almost flat.

🚚 The Operations team reported a slight increase in late deliveries.

🔍 **My Approach to the Problem**

Rather than jumping straight into solutions, I started by exploring potential root causes and identifying the data needed to validate my hypotheses.

Here are the key areas I focused on:

**1️⃣ Investigating Operational Issues**
- Analyzing late deliveries and identifying their potential causes.
- Investigating whether delays originate from restaurants, courier capacity, peak-hour demand, or technical issues.
- Evaluating customer feedback and survey responses.

**2️⃣ Customer Segmentation**
- Segmenting customers by geography and customer type.
- Investigating differences in behavior across payment methods.
- Identifying whether the retention decline is concentrated in specific customer segments.

**3️⃣ Connecting Product Metrics to Business Outcomes**
- Examining the relationship between acquisition, retention, and revenue.
- Investigating why higher customer acquisition isn't translating into proportional revenue growth.
- Looking beyond surface-level metrics to understand the underlying customer behavior.

💡 **My Key Takeaway**

A declining retention rate is a signal, not a root cause.

As Product Managers, our responsibility is to validate assumptions, investigate customer behavior, and prioritize solutions based on evidence rather than jumping to conclusions.

The goal isn't simply to acquire more customers. It's to understand what makes them return, deliver consistent value, and build sustainable business growth. 🎯
