# 🚀 Product Case Studies

### A hands-on Product Management learning journey

This repository contains **30 hypothetical Product Case Studies** created as part of my journey to strengthen my Product Management skills through practical problem-solving.

Instead of only studying frameworks and concepts, I'm using these cases to practice how I would approach real Product problems:

**Understand the problem → Analyze the data → Form hypotheses → Prioritize → Make decisions → Measure impact**

## 🧩 My Approach

For each case, I try to follow a structured Product process:

```text
Problem
   ↓
Context & Assumptions
   ↓
Funnel / Data Analysis
   ↓
Segmentation
   ↓
Hypotheses
   ↓
Validation
   ↓
Prioritization
   ↓
Solution / Action
   ↓
Success Metrics
   ↓
Learnings
```

The exact approach may change depending on the problem. The goal is not to force every case into the same framework, but to choose the **right tools for the problem**.

## 💭 Why This Series?

Product Management is not only about knowing frameworks.

It's about knowing **when to use them, why to use them, and how to make decisions when the information is incomplete.**

These case studies are my way of turning theory into practice and continuously improving my Product thinking.

> **30 cases. 30 problems. One goal: become a better Product thinker. 🚀**
